# TradePilot AI
## Product Vision

**Version:** 1.0

---

# Vision Statement

TradePilot AI exists to help investors make better decisions by making every recommendation transparent, explainable, educational, and statistically grounded.

Our goal is not to replace human judgment.

Our goal is to improve it.

TradePilot AI should become the world's most explainable AI investment analysis platform.

---

# Mission

Every recommendation should answer three questions:

1. What is happening?
2. Why is it happening?
3. How confident are we?

The application should never ask the user to trust a black box.

Instead, every recommendation must be backed by measurable evidence.

---

# Core Philosophy

Traditional trading applications focus on predictions.

TradePilot AI focuses on understanding.

Instead of telling the user what to do, the platform should teach the user how experienced investors think.

Confidence is earned through transparency.

---

# Product Principles

## 1. Explainability First

Every recommendation must be explainable.

Every score must be traceable.

Every calculation must be reproducible.

No hidden decision making.

---

## 2. Deterministic Recommendation Engine

The recommendation engine is always deterministic.

Given identical market data, it must always produce identical recommendations.

Recommendations are generated by statistical analysis and predefined algorithms.

Artificial Intelligence never generates the recommendation itself.

---

## 3. AI Explains — It Does Not Decide

Artificial Intelligence serves as an interpreter, educator and mentor.

Its responsibilities include:

- explaining recommendations
- teaching investment concepts
- answering questions
- simplifying complex statistical analysis
- comparing investment strategies

AI must never invent recommendation scores or replace deterministic calculations.

---

## 4. Trust Through Transparency

Every important value displayed to the user should answer:

- What is this?
- How was it calculated?
- Why does it matter?

Every significant metric should eventually include:

ⓘ Information

and

🤖 AI Mentor

---

## 5. Education is Part of the Product

TradePilot AI is not only an investment platform.

It is also a personalized learning platform.

The objective is that users become better investors over time.

The platform should teach:

- technical analysis
- fundamental analysis
- probability
- market psychology
- risk management
- statistical thinking

---

# Recommendation Architecture

TradePilot AI supports multiple investment strategies.

Each strategy analyzes the same market from a different perspective.

Different strategies may legitimately produce different recommendations.

This is expected and should be clearly communicated.

---

## Strategy 1

⚡ Trader

Purpose

Capture short-term opportunities.

Typical horizon

Minutes to several days.

Main evidence

- candle trend
- momentum
- RSI
- MACD
- VWAP
- volume
- breakout detection

---

## Strategy 2

📈 Swing Trader

Purpose

Capture medium-term market movements.

Typical horizon

Several days to several weeks.

Main evidence

- trend quality
- moving averages
- support
- resistance
- RSI
- MACD
- volume confirmation

---

## Strategy 3

🏛 Investor

Purpose

Identify high-quality long-term investment opportunities.

Typical horizon

Months to years.

Main evidence

- revenue growth
- earnings growth
- free cash flow
- debt
- valuation
- margins
- return on invested capital
- competitive advantage
- management quality
- innovation
- institutional ownership

---

# Strategy Summary

The application should present all strategies together.

Example

| Strategy | Recommendation | Confidence | Horizon |
|-----------|---------------|-----------:|----------|
| ⚡ Trader | Strong Buy | 91% | Hours–Days |
| 📈 Swing | Hold | 68% | Days–Weeks |
| 🏛 Investor | Buy | 94% | Months–Years |

The objective is not to produce one universal answer.

The objective is to help the user understand how different investment horizons lead to different conclusions.

---

# Explainability Layer

Every recommendation should be supported by an Explainability Layer.

This layer consists of two complementary components.

---

## ⓘ Information

Always deterministic.

Contains:

- mathematical explanation
- calculation method
- formulas
- historical comparison
- reliability calculation
- weighting explanation

No Artificial Intelligence is used.

---

## 🤖 AI Mentor

Conversational.

Built on top of deterministic evidence.

Capabilities include:

- explaining recommendations
- comparing indicators
- answering questions
- adapting explanations to user experience
- beginner mode
- intermediate mode
- professional mode

The AI Mentor always receives structured evidence generated by the recommendation engine.

# Design Philosophy

The interface should always answer:

What should I know?

before asking:

What should I do?

The application should reduce uncertainty by increasing understanding.

---

# Success Criteria

A successful recommendation is not one that is always correct.

A successful recommendation is one that is:

- statistically justified
- fully explainable
- transparent
- educational
- reproducible

---

# Guiding Principle

TradePilot AI does not aim to predict the future.

TradePilot AI aims to help investors understand probability, evidence, and risk so they can make better decisions with confidence.

---

# Product Motto

"Don't just tell me what to do.

Teach me why."
